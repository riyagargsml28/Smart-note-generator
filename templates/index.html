About Smart Notes Generator

        
An open-source AI study companion designed to bridge the gap between spoken academic lectures and structured revision mastery.

      


      

        
Project Mission

        
University students spend hundreds of hours listening to fast-paced lectures, often struggling to transcribe key formulas, technical definitions, and core arguments simultaneously. Smart Notes Generator automates this workflow: it cleans verbal fillers, identifies topic boundaries, synthesizes concise revision notes, and generates practice questions for active recall.


        
Architecture & Technology Stack

        

          Frontend: HTML5, Responsive Modern CSS, Vanilla ES6+
          Backend: Python 3, Flask, Werkzeug
          Speech-to-Text: OpenAI Whisper API
          AI LLM: GPT-4o-mini / Academic Semantic Engine
          Data Validation: Pydantic v2
          Database: SQLite3 with Full-Text Indexing
          PDF Generation: ReportLab Document Flowables

Academic Integrity & Security Notice

        

          AI-generated notes should be used as study aids and revision supplements. While the system adheres strictly to the transcript source material, always cross-reference critical formulas and definitions with your course textbook and professor's syllabus. Audio files and temporary uploads are automatically sanitized and never stored indefinitely.
